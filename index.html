About

I am a Class XI student and independent researcher working on edge-computing and sensor-fusion systems that address problems most commercial technology overlooks: smallholder farmers without reliable internet, informal workers without smartphones, and rural patients outside English-language health infrastructure.

My work sits at the intersection of embedded hardware, state estimation, and humanitarian-technology design constraints — particularly the constraint of building systems that must still function on the day the network fails, the phone battery dies, or the wearer cannot read the display.

Active Projects

Precision Agriculture

Offline-first decision support for smallholder irrigation

Edge sensing · state-space filtering · IMD forecast integration

A three-layer sensor-to-app system for the paddy–wheat rotations of Northwest India. Runs fully without a network as its baseline; opportunistically enhances decisions with India Meteorological Department forecast data when connectivity is available. Explainability layer renders reasoning traces in the 22 scheduled Indian languages without cloud inference.

Status: Peer-review, IEEE conference (2026)
Humanitarian Wearables

Phone-free wristband for cardiac and heat-stress early warning

Standalone edge inference · voice-first UX · cellular escalation

A wristband architected for India's informal workforce — outdoor labourers, rural elderly, post-cardiac patients — for whom paired-smartphone medical wearables are structurally inaccessible. Voice-first alerts in regional Indian languages; onboard cellular escalation without smartphone dependence.

Status: Peer-review, IEEE conference (2026)
